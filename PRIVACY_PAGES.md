## 📱 App Store Submission Steps

### Google Play Store

1. **Developer Console → App Content**
   - Add Privacy Policy URL
   - Complete Data Safety section
   - Match declarations with policy

2. **Data Safety Form**
   - Location: Precise location (GPS tracking app)
   - Device Info: Device IDs (for BMS app)
   - Select "Data is encrypted in transit"
   - Select "Users can request data deletion"

### Apple App Store

1. **App Store Connect → App Privacy**
   - Add Privacy Policy URL
   - Answer all data collection questions
   - Match with privacy policy content

2. **Privacy Questionnaire**
   - Location: Always (for GPS app)
   - Device ID: For analytics
   - Usage Data: Performance monitoring
